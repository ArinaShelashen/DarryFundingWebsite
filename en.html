🐰 What happened to the ears?

A brief overview of the diagnosis and treatment
Show/Hide ⌄

Disclaimer: we did NOT buy the rabbit, but adopted the only available boy from a shelter for our female rabbit, Monster. To breed rabbits with cute floppy ears, breeders reinforced the structure of their ears. Because of this, the cartilage and bones can be more sharply bent, and the ear canal can be narrowed (see right ear in the image). For some rabbits, this leads to poor ventilation, internal inflammation, and, over time, more serious problems.
We tried treatment with drops. Unfortunately, it did not help. Therefore, we had to resort to surgery to widen the ear canal (see left ear in the image). This had to be done as soon as possible, as the ears were looking quite poor, and fluid had already started collecting in the left one.
